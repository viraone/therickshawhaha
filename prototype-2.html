COMICS, TAKE NOTE

Fridays use a curated advance list. The first Sunday of each month is the walk-up open mic.

The signup list opens Friday night for the following week’s show and closes Thursday night. The producer curates the lineup from those submissions.

Show up, sign up, and go up. No advance list is required for the first-Sunday open mic.
